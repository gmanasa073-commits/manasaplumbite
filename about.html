Welcome to MANASA PLUMBITE!

MANASA PLUMBITE is an online food ordering platform designed to make delicious food easily accessible to everyone.

Our platform helps customers explore different food items, view prices, select their favourite dishes and add them to their shopping cart.

We aim to provide a simple, convenient and enjoyable food ordering experience with a variety of food choices.
